ML engineering agent · co-author, co-maintainer · 2024-now

AIDE

AIDE treats ML engineering as a tree search over code. It drafts a solution, runs it, debugs or improves the best node, and repeats until the budget runs out. I co-authored the 2025 paper and maintain the open-source release with the Weco team.
